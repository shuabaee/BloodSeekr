{% icon 'sparkle' 18 %} Good to know

  • O- is the universal red cell donor type, so it is always in demand for emergencies.
  • AB+ can receive red cells from every blood type.
  • Rh-negative patients can only receive Rh-negative red cells, so negative donors are especially valuable.

{% icon 'shield' 18 %} Important

The collecting facility always confirms blood typing, screening and crossmatch before a transfusion. Use this chart to understand matches, not to make medical decisions.
