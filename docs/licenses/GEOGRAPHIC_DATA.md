# Geographic data attribution

Country names/codes: ISO data distributed by pycountry (26.2.16).
World cities: GeoNames via geonamescache 2.0.0; major towns/cities, not every locality. https://www.geonames.org/ Licensed CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Province/state names: GeoNames admin1CodesASCII.txt, downloaded October 6, 2026. Same GeoNames attribution/license.
Philippines: names, hierarchy and city coordinates from psgc 2026.4.13.0 (community-maintained snapshot of PSA PSGC Q1 2026). https://github.com/fish-and-bear/psgc
PSA names/codes: Philippine Statistics Authority. Coordinates: OCHA HDX Philippines Subnational Administrative Boundaries, PSA/NAMRIA, CC BY-IGO: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-phl and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/
City points are approximate area centroids. Users can move the pin or use GPS for a specific collection point. Do not present these points as hospital coordinates.
Metro Cebu independent cities are grouped under Cebu for geographic selection; city PSGC IDs are retained. Other independent cities retain their dataset administrative area grouping.
Leaflet 1.9.4: BSD 2-Clause, bundled LICENSE retained. OpenStreetMap tiles require visible attribution, present in the map component. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Only a compact derived city dataset is bundled; no population, boundaries or full barangay datasets are redistributed.
